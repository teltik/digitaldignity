Eligibility

Who our programs serve

Digital Dignity helps people, households and community organizations get past the barriers that keep them offline. This page explains who our programs serve and the terms for taking part.

Who qualifies

Membership in Digital Dignity is free, with plans as low as $10 a month, and open to anyone who meets at least one of these. You qualify if one or more describes you:

  • I am a student, or a parent or guardian of a student who lives with me.
  • I need internet to look for work, do my job or take part in job training.
  • I represent a nonprofit, school, library or community organization.
  • The cost of internet is a barrier for my household, or affordable broadband is limited where I live.
  • My household needs a dependable connection for healthcare, such as video visits or managing prescriptions.

We may revise these statements and the proof we accept. The version on this page is always the current one.

How joining works

When you enroll with one of our community distribution partners, a local representative walks you through these statements. You read them, confirm that at least one is true for you, and mark the ones that apply. Confirming your eligibility makes you a member of Digital Dignity. Membership is free; plans run as low as $10 a month. We may ask you to show proof of eligibility at any time, and we may ask you to confirm your eligibility again once a year.

Member benefits

Membership in Digital Dignity provides more than connectivity. As a member, you receive:

  • Affordable internet. Wireless broadband internet at a cost well below typical retail rates, provided as a member benefit.
  • Setup support. Help placing, connecting and getting your router working. Community representatives and member support can walk you through setup and troubleshoot problems.
  • Dedicated member support. A member support team that understands Digital Dignity and helps you with your account, your equipment and your service.
  • Household tech help. Help connecting your household's devices to your internet, including phones, tablets and smart TVs.
  • Help with online services. When you need the internet for important tasks such as applying for benefits, scheduling healthcare or completing schoolwork, member support can guide you through the process.
  • Scam and fraud awareness. If you receive a suspicious message, call or email, we help you recognize and avoid scams and fraud that target families and seniors.
  • Community resource referrals. When you need help beyond connectivity, our community representatives can point you toward local resources and organizations that may be able to assist.
  • Equipment support. If your equipment stops working or needs replacement, Digital Dignity makes the process straightforward and supports you through it.

Member benefits are provided at Digital Dignity's discretion. Not all benefits are available in every location. Digital Dignity may add, change or discontinue member benefits at any time.

Where and how the programs can be used

  • Available in every US state except Alaska, plus Washington DC and Puerto Rico, wherever the network reaches. Check an address on our coverage map.
  • Each account serves one person, household or organization. Reselling the service, or sharing it with anyone outside your household or organization, is not allowed.
  • The service is meant mainly for learning, work and job training, healthcare, and everyday essential communication.

Your agreement

By checking the box at sign up, you confirm that at least one eligibility statement is true for you, and you agree to these terms and to the service terms you accept when you enroll.

What can end your participation

Your participation may end if you:

  • Give false or misleading information when you sign up or when asked to confirm eligibility.
  • Resell, share or pass the service on to others.
  • Use the service for anything unlawful or abusive.
  • Use the service in a way that harms the network or slows it down for other people.

Suspension and removal

Digital Dignity may suspend or end access to our programs if these terms are broken, if fraud or unauthorized use is found, or if you do not confirm your eligibility or provide requested proof when asked.

Your privacy

Enrolling is free and carries no dues or obligation beyond these terms. Service plans run as low as $10 a month.

To enroll you, confirm your eligibility and provide service, we collect your name, service address and contact details, the eligibility statements you select, and any proof of eligibility we ask for. We use this information only to provide your service, protect it, keep proof documents only as long as we need them, and never sell or rent it.

You can cancel at any time, at no cost, by emailing support@digitaldignity.online. If you cancel, we will help you move to another option or end service without penalty.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
