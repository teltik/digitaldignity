Our mission

Being offline today means a closed door.

School assignments, medical appointments, benefit applications and job interviews have all moved online. A household without a steady connection falls further behind every month.

The usual path to getting connected often shuts people out before they start: credit checks, autopay from a bank account, and sign up processes written for someone else. Digital Dignity exists to open that door.

We also believe a connection should not cost you your privacy. We keep as little information about the people we serve as possible, and we never sell it.

Who qualifies

Free membership, with plans as low as $10 a month, is open to you if one or more of these is true:

  • You are a student, or a parent or guardian of a student at home
  • You need internet to find work, do your job or train for one
  • You represent a nonprofit, school, library or community organization
  • Internet cost is a barrier, or affordable options are limited where you live
  • You need a dependable connection for healthcare

Your information

To enroll you, confirm your eligibility and keep you connected, we collect your name, service address, contact details and eligibility answers, plus any proof of eligibility you are asked to provide. We use it only to provide your service. We never sell or rent personal information.

Cancel anytime

You can end your enrollment at any time, at no cost, by emailing support@digitaldignity.online. We will help you move to another option or cancel without penalty.

FAQ

Common questions

Do not see your question here? Send it through the form below and we will answer it.

Who can get service through Digital Dignity?

Service is available to anyone who confirms at least one of our eligibility statements. Membership is free and plans as low as $10 a month. Many members find us through a school, library or community organization, and you can also join directly.

What does it cost?

Enrolling is free. plans as low as $10 a month, and you do not need a bank account or autopay to sign up.

What equipment do I need?

Just your own phones, tablets or computers. We provide a home internet router that is set up before it reaches you. Plug it in and connect your devices.

Is there an installation appointment?

No. Service runs over a 5G wireless network, so there are no cables to run and no technician visit.

Will it work at my address?

Use the coverage map above for a quick check. Before anything ships, we confirm coverage at your exact address.

How can my organization partner with you?

Schools, libraries, congregations, community centers and social service agencies can refer the families they serve. Choose your organization type in the form below and we will set up a call.

What information does Digital Dignity keep about me?

What we need to enroll you, confirm your eligibility and provide service: your name, service address, contact details, the eligibility statements you selected, and any proof of eligibility you are asked to provide. We use it only to provide your service, keep proof documents only as long as needed, and never sell or rent it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time and at no cost. Email support@digitaldignity.online and we will help you move to another option or cancel without penalty.
