Our mission

Being offline today means a closed door.

School assignments, medical appointments, benefit applications and job interviews have all moved online. A household without a steady connection falls further behind every month.

The usual path to getting connected often shuts people out before they start: credit checks, autopay from a bank account, and sign up processes written for someone else. Digital Dignity exists to open that door.

FAQ

Common questions

Do not see your question here? Send it through the form below and we will answer it.

Who can get service through Digital Dignity?

Households in our service area that need a reliable, affordable connection, especially students, seniors and families who have been left offline. Many people reach us through a school, library or community organization, but you can also contact us directly.

Do I need a credit check or a bank account?

No. There is no credit check, and you do not need a bank account or autopay to sign up.

What equipment do I need?

Just your own phones, tablets or computers. We provide a home internet router that is set up before it reaches you. Plug it in and connect your devices.

Is there an installation appointment?

No. Service runs over a 5G wireless network, so there are no cables to run and no technician visit.

Will it work at my address?

Use the coverage map above for a quick check. Before anything ships, we confirm coverage at your exact address.

How can my organization partner with you?

Schools, libraries, congregations, community centers and social service agencies can refer the families they serve. Choose your organization type in the form below and we will set up a call.

What happens to my information?

We use it only to respond to you and to provide service. We never sell or share it.
