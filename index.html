A dependable connection belongs in every home.

Digital Dignity helps families, seniors and students who have been left offline get reliable, affordable home internet. No credit check. No bank account required. Real people to call when something goes wrong.

Get in touch Partner with us

Being offline today means a closed door.

School assignments, medical appointments, benefit applications and job interviews have all moved online. A household without a steady connection falls further behind every month.

The usual path to getting connected often shuts people out before they start: credit checks, autopay from a bank account, and sign up processes written for someone else. Digital Dignity exists to open that door.

Who we serve

Neighbors across New Jersey who need a connection to learn, stay healthy and stay close to the people they love.

Students

Finishing homework at home instead of in a parking lot outside a library or restaurant.

Seniors

Seeing a doctor by video, managing prescriptions, and staying in touch with children and grandchildren.

Families

Applying for work, handling benefits and paperwork, and keeping a busy household running.

How we help

We work with established carrier partners to bring home internet to households that need it, and we stay with them after it is plugged in.

Home internet that fits

5G home internet delivered over a national wireless network. No cable lines, no installation appointment, no drilling.

Ready on day one

Each home receives a router that is set up in advance. Plug it in, connect your devices, and you are online.

Help from real people

Questions go to a person who will walk you through it in plain language, as many times as it takes.
